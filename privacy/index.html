This Privacy Policy explains how LockIn collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the LockIn mobile app and the lockinfit.app website. It is written to be readable. If anything is unclear, email us at privacy@lockinfit.app.

The short version

  • We collect your email, username, and workout activity (sets, sessions, coins) so the app works.
  • Your data is stored in Canada (Montreal) on Supabase. We do not sell it. We do not run ads. We do not use analytics SDKs.
  • Weather and push notifications are handled by Apple directly. We never see your location.
  • You can delete your account in-app at any time. Deletion is permanent.
  • You have rights under GDPR, CCPA, PIPEDA, and similar laws. Email us to use them.
  • LockIn is for users 13 and older.

1. Who we are

LockIn ("LockIn," "we," "us," or "our") is a gamified fitness app operated from Toronto, Ontario, Canada.

For all privacy-related questions, requests, or complaints, contact us at privacy@lockinfit.app.

2. Information we collect

We collect only what we need to run LockIn. Specifically:

2.1 Account information

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address — used to sign in and to contact you about your account.
  • Username — the name shown to your friends and group members in the app.
  • Password — stored only in hashed form by our authentication service (Supabase Auth). We never see your plain-text password.

2.2 Workout and gameplay data

As you use LockIn, we store the activity that drives the in-app coin economy and your studio:

  • Gym sessions — when you start and end a workout, and whether it was a solo or group session.
  • Sets completed — how many sets you finished per session. We do not record which specific exercises you performed.
  • Coin transactions — coins earned per set (including rescue sets), coins spent on characters, and the timestamp of each transaction. This is the source of truth for your Weekly Report stats.
  • Characters owned — which characters you've purchased and have placed in your studio.

2.3 Social data

  • Friend relationships — usernames you've sent friend requests to, accepted from, or are connected with.
  • Group session participation — which group sessions you've hosted or joined, and the participants in those sessions.
  • Notifications — records of friend requests received and group invitations sent or received.

2.4 Subscription information

If you subscribe to LockIn Pro, the subscription is processed by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never see or store your credit card, payment method, or billing address. We only receive a confirmation from Apple that you have an active subscription (an "entitlement"), which lets us unlock Pro features for your account.

2.5 Push notification token (if you enable notifications)

If you grant permission for push notifications, your device generates an Apple Push Notification service (APNs) token. We store this token so we can deliver notifications — for example, when a friend invites you to a group session. If you turn off notifications, this token is no longer used. APNs is operated by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.

2.6 Location data (if you enable weather rewards)

LockIn's weather reward feature uses Apple's WeatherKit. When you enable it, your device requests weather data based on your current location. This happens entirely on your device. Your location is never sent to LockIn's servers and is never stored by us. Apple receives the location request to return the weather for your area, subject to Apple's privacy policy.

2.7 Technical and security logs

Our authentication and database provider (Supabase) automatically logs limited technical information for security and reliability — for example, IP addresses and sign-in timestamps. These logs are retained by Supabase under their own data retention policies (see supabase.com/privacy). We do not use this information for marketing or advertising.

2.8 What we do NOT collect

We want to be explicit about what we do not do:

  • We do not use third-party analytics SDKs (no PostHog, Mixpanel, Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or similar).
  • We do not use third-party crash reporting SDKs (no Crashlytics, Sentry, or similar). Apple's standard Xcode Organizer crash reports may be available to us if you opt in to share diagnostics with developers in iOS Settings.
  • We do not run advertising. We do not use advertising identifiers (IDFA).
  • We do not collect your contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or HealthKit data.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • The lockinfit.app website does not set cookies and does not run analytics.

3. How we use your information

We use the information described above only to:

  • Run the core app — track your workouts, award coins, sync your studio, manage friends and group sessions.
  • Authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • Send you push notifications you've opted in to.
  • Provide LockIn Pro features to subscribers.
  • Respond to support requests and privacy-related requests.
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud or abuse.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising profiling.

4. Legal bases for processing (EU / UK / EEA users)

If you are in the European Union, the United Kingdom, or the European Economic Area, we rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR and UK GDPR:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the LockIn service you signed up for (account, workouts, social features, subscription).
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the service secure, prevent abuse, and improve the app, where these interests are not overridden by your rights.
  • Consent — for optional features like push notifications and WeatherKit location, which you explicitly enable.
  • Legal obligation — when required to respond to lawful requests from authorities.

You may withdraw consent for opt-in features at any time in your device settings.

5. How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not rent it. We do not share it for marketing.

We share information only with the following parties, and only as needed to run the app:

5.1 Supabase

Supabase is our backend infrastructure provider. They host our database and authentication system in ca-central-1 (Montreal, Canada), on AWS. Supabase acts as our data processor under a Data Processing Addendum that requires them to protect your data and use it only on our instructions. Learn more in Supabase's Privacy Policy.

5.2 Apple

Apple operates the App Store, StoreKit (subscriptions), APNs (push notifications), and WeatherKit (weather). When you use these features, Apple processes the relevant data under Apple's Privacy Policy. We do not have access to your payment information or your device location.

5.3 Other friends and group members

By design, your username is visible to other LockIn users when:

  • They search for you to send a friend request.
  • You appear in their friend list (after a friend connection is accepted).
  • You join a group workout session they are part of.

Your email address is never shown to other users. Only your username is visible.

5.4 Legal disclosures

We may disclose information if required by law, valid legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of LockIn, our users, or the public. We will challenge requests that we believe are overbroad or improper.

6. Where your data is stored

Your LockIn account data is stored in Montreal, Canada (Supabase region ca-central-1, hosted on AWS). Canada is recognized by the European Commission as providing an adequate level of data protection for personal data transferred from the EU/EEA under PIPEDA's commercial sector framework, which simplifies international transfers.

Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Database access is protected by row-level security policies that restrict each user to their own data.

7. International data transfers

LockIn is available worldwide except mainland China. Regardless of where you live, your account data is stored in Canada. If you are in a jurisdiction other than Canada, your data will be transferred to and processed in Canada.

For users in the EU/UK/EEA, this transfer is supported by the European Commission's adequacy decision for Canada. For users in other jurisdictions, equivalent safeguards apply through our agreements with Supabase.

8. How long we keep your data

  • Active accounts — we retain your account data for as long as your account is active.
  • Deleted accounts — when you delete your account, all your personal data across our 7 user-data tables (account, characters owned, gym sessions, session participants, coin transactions, friendships, notifications) is permanently deleted via cascade. Backups may retain residual copies for a limited period before being overwritten in normal backup rotation.
  • Security logs — short-term technical logs are kept by Supabase under their retention policies.
  • Legal holds — we may retain limited information longer if required by law (for example, to comply with tax or fraud-prevention obligations).

9. Your privacy rights

You have rights over your personal data. The exact rights depend on where you live, but we apply the following protections globally as a baseline:

9.1 Universal rights (everyone)

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction — ask us to fix data that's wrong or incomplete.
  • Deletion — delete your account and your data, at any time, in the app.
  • Withdraw consent — turn off optional features like push notifications or weather permissions in iOS Settings.

9.2 GDPR / UK GDPR rights (EU / UK / EEA users)

In addition to the universal rights above, you have the right to:

  • Restrict processing in certain circumstances.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Data portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority. In the UK, that is the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). In EU member states, contact your national authority.

We do not engage in automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects.

9.3 CCPA / CPRA rights (California residents)

If you are a California resident:

  • Right to know what personal information we have collected about you, the sources, the purposes, and any third parties we share it with.
  • Right to delete your personal information.
  • Right to correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Right to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" of personal information — we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising, so this right does not apply in practice.
  • Right to non-discrimination — we will not deny services, charge different prices, or provide a different level of service because you exercised these rights.

We do not sell personal information. We do not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. We have not done so in the past 12 months.

9.4 PIPEDA rights (Canadian residents)

If you are in Canada, you have rights under the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA), including the right to access your personal information, request correction, and withdraw consent. You may also file a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

9.5 Other jurisdictions

If you are in a jurisdiction with privacy laws not specifically listed above (for example, Brazil's LGPD, Australia's Privacy Act, or other regional laws), we will honor analogous rights to the maximum extent practical. Contact us and we will work with you in good faith.

9.6 How to exercise your rights

Email privacy@lockinfit.app with your request. To protect your privacy, we may need to verify your identity (for example, by confirming the email associated with your account). We aim to respond within 30 days, as required by applicable law. If we need more time, we will tell you why.

For deletion, the fastest way is to use Account → Delete Account in the app, which is immediate and permanent.

10. Account deletion

You can delete your LockIn account at any time:

  1. Open the LockIn app.
  2. Go to Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm.

When you delete your account, the following data is permanently removed:

  • Your account record (email, username).
  • Your authentication credentials.
  • All characters you've purchased.
  • All gym sessions you've hosted or joined.
  • All coin transactions tied to your account.
  • All your friend relationships.
  • All notifications sent to or from your account.

Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Backups may retain residual copies for a limited period, after which they are overwritten in normal backup rotation.

If you've subscribed to LockIn Pro, deleting your LockIn account does not automatically cancel your Apple subscription. To cancel the subscription, go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

11. Security

We protect your data using:

  • TLS encryption for all data in transit between your device and our servers.
  • Encryption at rest provided by Supabase / AWS infrastructure.
  • Row-level security policies on every database table, so each user can only access their own rows.
  • Hashed passwords — we never see your plain-text password.
  • Limited access — only the LockIn team accesses production data, and only when necessary to operate the service.

No system is perfectly secure. If you become aware of a security issue with LockIn, please email privacy@lockinfit.app. We take responsible disclosure seriously.

12. Children's privacy

LockIn is intended for users aged 13 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with a higher digital consent age (typically 14, 15, or 16 depending on the country), you must meet your local minimum age to use LockIn.

If you believe a child under the applicable minimum age has created a LockIn account, email privacy@lockinfit.app and we will delete the account and associated data promptly.
